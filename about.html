About Me

My name is Roniel Nyke Gonzales. I am a BS Information Technology student (Year 2, Section B) at West Visayas State University - Lambunao Campus, and a self-driven web developer from Brgy. Sibaguan, Lambunao, Iloilo. I do not build simple demonstrations. I build complete systems that real users can use, such as code checkers, proposal evaluators, marketplaces and gamified learning platforms, with sign-in, saved data and clear explanations.

I care about three things: a professional UX/UI design, smooth JavaScript and CSS animation, and zero errors. Every error I find is fixed right away, before I deliver. My systems are hosted on Vercel, many of them use Firebase, and I protect my work with a real license.
